Imagemer Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 21, 2026 Last updated: October 29, 2026

Welcome to Imagemer. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information, particularly regarding the photos you upload and our use of Artificial Intelligence (AI).

1. Information We Collect

To provide the Imagemer experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Images and Photos: When you use our core features, you upload photos from your device's gallery or camera.
  • Account Information: Imagemer creates an anonymous guest account automatically. If you choose to link your account, we collect basic profile information (such as your email address) via Google Auth.
  • Device and Technical Identifiers: When you launch the app, we generate/collect a unique, anonymous device identifier (such as an app-specific instance ID or Android ID). This is used strictly for fraud prevention, managing one-time free credit allocations, and rate limiting. It is not linked to your personal identity.
  • Purchase History: If you buy credit packs, your transaction data is processed securely through RevenueCat and Google Play. We do not collect or store your credit card information.
  • App Usage Events: We record which screens you open and which themes you view, to understand and improve the app. These events are linked to your anonymous account, are never sold or used for advertising, and are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Content Reports: If you report a generated image, we keep the reason you select, any details you add, and the theme concerned, for moderation purposes. These report records are retained even after you delete your account.
  • No Biometric Data: We do not extract, create or store biometric identifiers, face templates or facial recognition data. Your photos are processed only to generate the image you requested.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • AI Image Generation: The photos you upload are used strictly to generate themed avatars and images based on your requests.
  • App Functionality: To manage your credits, save your generated images to your account history, and maintain your session.

3. Third-Party Services and AI Processing

To generate your images, Imagemer uses third-party AI services. By using the app, you understand that your uploaded photos are sent to these providers temporarily for processing:

  • Google Gemini API: We use Gemini as our AI engine to analyze your uploaded photos and generate new themed images.
  • Supabase: We use Supabase for secure backend database and authentication hosting.
  • RevenueCat: Used for processing in-app purchases and managing your credit balance.

4. Data Retention

  • Uploaded Photos: Your original photos are not stored. They are held only for the few seconds required to generate your image, then permanently deleted from our servers automatically, on every attempt, including failed ones. They are never used to train AI models.
  • Generated Images: Your generated images are saved to your account so you can view them in your history. They remain until you delete them or your account.
  • Account Data: Your credit balance, purchase records and generation history are kept for as long as your account exists.

5. Account and Data Deletion (Your Rights)

You have full control over your data. You can request the complete deletion of your account, including all uploaded photos, generated images, and associated email data at any time.

  • How to delete: In the Imagemer app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes your generated images, credit balance, purchase history and account. It cannot be undone, and any remaining credits are forfeited and cannot be refunded.
  • Alternatively, email savas@kzngames.me with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and we will process it within 7 days.
  • Note: To prevent abuse of our free trial credits, we retain a single anonymous device identifier after deletion. It is not linked to you, your account, or any personal information, it only records that this device has already claimed a free trial.

6. Children’s Privacy

Imagemer is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are under 13 years old, do not use or provide any information on this app. If we learn that we have collected personal data from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will promptly delete that information and the associated account. If you believe we might have any information from or about a child under 13, please contact us at savas@kzngames.me.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
